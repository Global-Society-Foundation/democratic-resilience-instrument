# Dimension 4: Democratic Stress Tolerance

*Dispositional/behavioural dimension. Covers willingness to defend democratic norms under threat, resistance to authoritarian appeals under crisis framing, tolerance for political adversaries and outgroups, capacity to maintain civic engagement under conditions of political disillusionment, and willingness to accept democratic outcomes that conflict with one's own preferences.*

|                       |                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Two forms**         | Form A and Form B are parallel forms measuring the same constructs in different words. Form order is randomised per participant and fixed across two waves, pre- and post-treatment. |
| **Two levels**        | Each construct can be tested for national-level or EU-level programmes.                                                                                                              |
| **Response scale**    | 5-point Likert scale items.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Measurement model** | Formative — the five constructs are distinct components, not interchangeable indicators.                                                                                             |

The scenarios in this dimension derive their threat model from Bauer and Becker's (2020) four strategies of anti-pluralist state transformation, inverted from what a government does into what a citizen can perceive.

---

## 4.1 Willingness to defend democratic norms under threat

> [!WARNING]
> **Reverse-scored** — recode `6 − raw score`.

Anchor family: **Support** — *strongly oppose · oppose · neither support nor oppose · support · strongly support*.

### 4.1.1 — National level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** The party you support proposes a reform that would remove the ability of courts to limit the power of the government. Supporters argue it would make decision-making more efficient. To what extent would you oppose or support this proposal?

**Form B.** A court rules against a major policy reform proposed by the party you support. However, the party leader says the ruling should be ignored. To what extent would you oppose or support the reform?

### 4.1.2 — EU level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** The party you support proposes a reform to limit the European Court of Justice's ability to review and overturn decisions made by EU institutions. Supporters argue it would make decision-making more efficient. To what extent would you oppose or support this proposal?

**Form B.** The European Court of Justice rules against a major policy reform backed by the political group you support. However, the political group leader says the ruling should be ignored. To what extent would you oppose or support the reform?

## 4.2 Resistance to authoritarian appeals under crisis framing

> [!WARNING]
> **Reverse-scored** — recode `6 − raw score`.

Anchor family: **Support**.

### 4.2.1 — National level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** An attack from outside the country damages the power network, causing blackouts across the country. The leader proposes emergency powers for six months: during that time the government could make decisions without a vote in parliament, and courts could not check whether those decisions were legal. The leader says this is needed to act quickly. To what extent would you oppose or support this proposal?

**Form B.** During a major migration crisis in your country, the government you support has proposed a temporary suspension of all foreign media outlets, arguing it is necessary to limit the spread of misinformation. To what extent would you oppose or support this suspension?

### 4.2.2 — EU level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** An attack from outside the EU damages the power network, causing blackouts across member states. The European Commission proposes to hold emergency powers for six months: during that time it could make decisions without a vote in the European Parliament, and courts could not check whether those decisions were legal. The European Commission President says this is needed to act quickly. To what extent would you oppose or support this proposal?

**Form B.** During a major cross-border migration crisis affecting several EU states, the political group you support proposes a temporary suspension of non-EU media outlets, arguing it is necessary to limit the spread of misinformation. To what extent would you oppose or support this suspension?

## 4.3 Tolerance for political adversaries and outgroups

*Not reverse-scored. Anchor family: Agreement.*

> **Same item for both the national and EU questionnaires.**

**Form A.** "I would be comfortable having a close friend or family member who supports a political group I strongly disagree with."

**Form B.** "I support the right of the political groups I strongly disagree with to hold peaceful public demonstrations."

---

## 4.4 Capacity to maintain civic engagement under conditions of political disillusionment

*Not reverse-scored. Anchor family: Agreement.*

> **Same item for both the national and EU questionnaires.**

**Form A.** "Even if I felt my vote or voice made little difference, I would continue participating in civic or political life."

**Form B.** "If a policy reform that mattered a lot to me was rejected despite my efforts to promote it, I would still engage on that issue in the future."

---

## 4.5 Willingness to accept democratic outcomes that conflict with one's own preferences

*Not reverse-scored. Anchor family: Agreement.*

### 4.5.1 — National level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** In the latest national elections, the candidate you supported lost by less than 1% of the vote. Independent election observers say the results are accurate. The candidate says the election was corrupted and refuses to concede. "I would accept the election result as legitimate."

**Form B.** In a referendum, your region votes on whether to become independent from your country. The outcome goes against the option you supported, by a narrow margin. Independent observers say the vote was conducted fairly. "I would accept the referendum result as legitimate."

### 4.5.2 — EU level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** In the European Parliament elections, the candidate you supported lost by less than 1% of the vote. Independent election observers say the results are accurate. The candidate says the election was corrupted and refuses to concede. "I would accept the election result as legitimate."

**Form B.** In a referendum, your country votes on whether to leave the European Union. The outcome goes against the option you supported, by a narrow margin. Independent observers say the vote was conducted fairly. "I would accept the referendum result as legitimate."
