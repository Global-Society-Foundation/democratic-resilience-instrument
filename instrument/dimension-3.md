# Dimension 3: Democratic Political Culture

*Attitudinal/normative dimension. Covers consistency of commitment to democratic norms across partisan contexts, rejection of anti-democratic alternatives, positive affective orientation toward democracy as a system of government, sense of belonging to a shared political community, and openness to opposing views.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Two forms** | Form A and Form B are parallel forms measuring the same constructs in different words. Form order is randomised per participant and fixed across two waves, pre- and post-treatment. |
| **Two levels** | Each construct can be tested for national-level or EU-level programmes. |
| **Response scale** | 5-point Likert scale items and 0–10 thermometer. |
| **Measurement model** | Formative — the five constructs are distinct components, not interchangeable indicators. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Several items in this dimension constitute data *revealing political opinions* under Article 9 GDPR. See [`administration-and-validation.md`](../methodology/administration-and-validation.md) §9 before fielding.

---

## 3.1 Consistency of commitment to democratic norms across partisan contexts

> [!WARNING]
> **Reverse-scored** — recode `6 − raw score`.

Anchor family: **Support** — *strongly oppose · oppose · neither support nor oppose · support · strongly support*.

> Inspired by: Tilley et al. (2026). Partisan (In)Tolerance and Affective Polarization. *British Journal of Political Science*, 56, e36. [doi:10.1017/S0007123426101550](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007123426101550)

### 3.1.1 — National level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** A party you support wants to redraw the map of voting areas. It says the current map is out of date. Independent experts say the new map would make it easier for that party to win seats. To what extent would you oppose or support this?

**Form B.** A government led by a party you support wants to cut the money given to the national public broadcaster. It says the broadcaster wastes money and should pay for itself. The broadcaster has recently run several reports criticising the government. To what extent would you oppose or support this?

### 3.1.2 — EU level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** A political group you support proposes changing how the European Parliament seats are allocated after elections, arguing the current arrangement does not reflect recent population shifts. Analysts estimate the change would increase this group's influence in the Parliament. To what extent would you support this proposal?

**Form B.** A political group you support proposes cutting funding to a European public-interest news organisation, arguing EU funds would be better spent elsewhere. This organisation has recently published critical reports about this political group. To what extent would you support this funding cut?

## 3.2 Rejection of anti-democratic alternatives

> [!WARNING]
> **Reverse-scored** — recode `6 − raw score`.

Anchor family: **Agreement** — *strongly disagree · disagree · neither agree nor disagree · agree · strongly agree*.

### 3.2.1 — National level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** "My country would be better run by a strong leader who can make important decisions without having to get the parliament's approval each time." To what extent do you agree with this statement?

**Form B.** "If my country faced a serious emergency, such as a pandemic, it would be acceptable to delay the next national election until the emergency was over." To what extent do you agree with this statement?

### 3.2.2 — EU level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** "The EU would work better if the European Commission could make important decisions without needing the European Parliament's approval." To what extent do you agree with this statement?

**Form B.** "If Europe faced a serious emergency, such as a pandemic, it would be acceptable to delay the next European Parliament elections until the emergency was over." To what extent do you agree with this statement?

## 3.3 Positive affective orientation toward democracy as a system of government

*Not reverse-scored. 0–10 thermometer; only the endpoints are shown to respondents.*

### 3.3.1 — National level

**Form A.** Using a scale from 0 (not positively) to 10 (extremely positively), how do you feel about democracy as a system of national government?

**Form B.** Using a scale from 0 (not favourably) to 10 (extremely favourably), how do you feel about democracy as a way of governing your country?

### 3.3.2 — EU level

**Form A.** Using a scale from 0 (not positively) to 10 (extremely positively), how do you feel about democracy as a system for governing the European Union?

**Form B.** Using a scale from 0 (not favourably) to 10 (extremely favourably), how do you feel about democracy as a way of governing the European Union?

---

## 3.4 Sense of belonging to a shared political community

*Not reverse-scored. Anchor family: Agreement.*

### 3.4.1 — National level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** "Despite our differences, I feel connected to my fellow citizens as part of the same political community." To what extent do you agree with this statement?

**Form B.** "Even when I disagree with the decisions made in my country, I still feel like I belong here." To what extent do you agree with this statement?

### 3.4.2 — EU level · 5-point Likert

**Form A.** "Despite our differences, I feel connected to people in other European countries as part of one community." To what extent do you agree with this statement?

**Form B.** "Even when I disagree with the decisions made at the EU level, I still feel part of a European community." To what extent do you agree with this statement?

---

## 3.5 Openness to opposing views

*Not reverse-scored. 0–10 thermometer, anchors* not at all – completely *in both forms.*

> **Same item for both the national and EU questionnaires.**

**Form A.** Using a scale from 0 (not at all) to 10 (completely), how willing are you to talk with someone whose views on politics are very different from your own?

**Form B.** Using a scale from 0 (not at all) to 10 (completely), when you come across an opinion about politics that you disagree with, how much do you try to understand why the person holds it?
