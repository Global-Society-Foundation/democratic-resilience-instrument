# Changelog

This file keeps track of all notable changes made to the DRI.

**Rules**

- Log every change to items, scoring, dimensions or methodology.
- One dated entry per version, newest first.
- Group changes under: Added, Changed, Fixed, Removed.
- Name what changed, in which file, and what prompted it.
- Never edit or delete a past entry. Corrections are new entries.

See [`README.md`](README.md#versioning) for what a MAJOR, MINOR or PATCH bump means for score comparability.

---

## [v1.0] — 17 September 2026

**Added**

- Initial public release. Twenty items across four dimensions, in Forms A and B, at national and EU level.
- Methodology package: administration and validation, scale standardisation, bias mitigation, validation roadmap.
- Working paper deposited on Zenodo, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.22810038](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22810038). Presented at the 3rd Conference of the European Network for Digital Democracy (EDDY), Vienna, 21–22 September 2026.
