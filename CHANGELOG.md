# Changelog

This file keeps track of all notable changes made to the DRI.

**Rules**

- Log every change to items, scoring, dimensions or methodology.
- One dated entry per version, newest first.
- Group changes under: Added, Changed, Fixed, Removed.
- Name what changed, in which file, and what prompted it.
- Never edit or delete a past entry. Corrections are new entries.

See [`README.md`](README.md#versioning) for what a MAJOR, MINOR or PATCH bump means for score comparability.

---

## [v1.1] — 26 October 2026

**Added**

- Working paper v1.1 deposited on Zenodo as a new version of the same record, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23674908](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23674908). The v1.0 DOI remains valid and still resolves to v1.0.

**Changed**

- Scope widened from "civic technology" to "civic initiatives" (e.g. civic education programmes, citizens' assemblies, participatory apps), in the working paper and its title, in [`README.md`](README.md), and in [`methodology/administration-and-validation.md`](methodology/administration-and-validation.md). The DRI applies to civic initiatives generally, not only digital ones. Items, response scales, scoring and dimensions are unchanged, so scores remain comparable with v1.0.
- Recommended citation and BibTeX in [`README.md`](README.md) now point to v1.1 and name The Global Society Foundation as publisher, matching the working paper.
- Language section of [`methodology/administration-and-validation.md`](methodology/administration-and-validation.md) (§4.5) no longer names a version number.

**Fixed**

- In-text citation year for Holloway & Manwaring in the working paper, from 2022 to 2023, to match the reference list.

---

## [v1.0] — 17 September 2026

**Added**

- Initial public release. Twenty items across four dimensions, in Forms A and B, at national and EU level.
- Methodology package: administration and validation, scale standardisation, bias mitigation, validation roadmap.
- Working paper deposited on Zenodo, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.22810038](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22810038). Presented at the 3rd Conference of the European Network for Digital Democracy (EDDY), Vienna, 21–22 September 2026.
